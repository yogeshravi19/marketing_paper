## 2. WHY THESE 17 PAPERS WERE SELECTED (2–3 Precise Sentences Per Paper)

1. **[Azhar & Juidette (2025)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Azhar_Juidette_2025_Impact_Digital_Marketing_Customer_Acquisition_PRISMA.pdf)** *(Outcome: Customer Acquisition)*: This 2025 PRISMA systematic review screens 263 scholarly records (2015–2024) and proves that only **12 empirical studies globally** examine digital marketing specifically for **customer acquisition** (as opposed to awareness, intention, or retention). Crucially, it shows that only **1 qualitative study** (`Sedalo et al., 2022`) examined SMEs and only **1 single-channel study** (`Zheng et al., 2022`) linked engagement to acquisition, giving our thesis an unassailable, citation-backed statement of the research gap.
2. **[De (2025)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/WhatsApp_Business_India_Small_Business_2025_2502.10913.pdf)** *(Outcome: First-Time Order Completion & Repeat Business in Indian Small Businesses)*: Published at ACM CHI '25, this study investigates how Indian small business owners actually acquire and convert customers across digital touchpoints. It proves that while social media (Instagram/Facebook reels and ads) serves as an upper-funnel discovery touchpoint, **Direct Conversational Touchpoints (WhatsApp Business catalogs, 1-on-1 chat, status updates, and UPI links)** are the indispensable trust-building touchpoints that convert Indian leads into first-time buyers.
3. **[Dwivedi et al. (2023)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Dwivedi_et_al_2023_MASEM_Social_Commerce_Adoption.pdf)** *(Outcome: Both Purchase Intention [$R^2 = 48.5\%$] AND Actual Purchase Behavior [$R^2 = 29.2\%$])*: Synthesizing 68 empirical studies via Meta-Analytic SEM (MASEM), this paper proves that **Purchase Intention does not automatically equal Actual Purchase Behavior ($\beta = 0.28$)**. More importantly for our conceptual model, it discovers an emergent direct pathway in which **Trust ($\beta = 0.33, p < 0.01$)** and **Emotional Support ($\beta = 0.16, p < 0.01$)** directly drive **actual conversion behavior**, justifying why our framework must measure both intention and actual first purchase.
4. **[Buhalis & Volchek (2021)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Buhalis_Volchek_2021_Bridging_Marketing_Theory_Big_Data_Attribution.pdf)** *(Outcome: Multi-Touch Conversion Attribution)*: Synthesizing 62 marketing attribution studies, this paper builds the definitive taxonomy of multi-touch attribution (covering Logistic Regression, Machine Learning, Shapley Values, and Markov Chains). It explicitly diagnoses our core **methodological gap (pp. 31–39)**: algorithmic attribution models rely purely on behavioral click counts (`touchpoint exposure → conversion`) while ignoring the customer's psychological state transitions (`engagement → trust → perceived value`).
5. **[Bleier, Harmeling & Palmatier (2019)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Bleier_et_al_2019_Creating_Effective_Online_Customer_Experiences.pdf)** *(Outcome: Both Purchase Intention [$N = 10,470$] AND Actual Product Sales in an Amazon Field Experiment)*: Published in the *Journal of Marketing*, this study maps 13 specific digital touchpoint design elements (e.g., star ratings, conversational linguistic style, lifestyle photos, video) to 4 customer experience dimensions (`Informativeness`, `Entertainment`, `Social Presence`, `Sensory Appeal`). Crucially for SMEs, it proves that **less-trusted / unfamiliar brands** gain significantly higher conversion lifts from social presence, sensory appeal, and informative touchpoints because these cues reduce uncertainty for first-time buyers.
6. **[Moliner & Tortosa-Edo (2024)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Moliner_Tortosa-Edo_2024_Touchpoints_Customer_Journey.pdf)** *(Outcome: Cognitive & Affective Online Customer Experience and e-Satisfaction)*: This 2024 study cleanly separates **External (Brand-Owned) Digital Touchpoints** from **Internal (Independent/Earned Peer) Digital Touchpoints** across the **Pre-Purchase, Purchase, and Post-Purchase stages**. It provides a validated multi-item questionnaire (Table 2, p. 6) and proves that independent peer touchpoints (reviews, forums, peer social media) exert a stronger effect on cognitive and affective evaluation than brand-controlled touchpoints alone.
7. **[Hallikainen, Alamäki & Laukkanen (2019)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Hallikainen_et_al_2019_Individual_Preferences_Digital_Touchpoints_LCA.pdf)** *(Outcome: Perceived Importance/Preference of 9 Specific Digital Touchpoints in Purchasing, $N = 2,348$)*: Using Latent Class Analysis across 2,348 consumers, this study classifies 9 digital touchpoints into **Functional** (Search Engine, Brand Website, Email, Online Chat), **Social** (Social Networks, Photo Communities like Instagram, Video Communities like YouTube), and **Community** (Blogs, Discussion Forums). It proves that across all consumer segments, **functional touchpoints (Search $M = 3.98$, Website $M = 3.86$) are preferred for actual purchasing over social media ($M = 1.98\text{–}2.46$)**, supporting our hypothesis that social media initiates engagement while functional/conversational touchpoints close the first sale.
8. **[Erkan & Evans (2018)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Erkan_Evans_2018_Social_Media_vs_Shopping_Websites_eWOM_Purchase_Intention.pdf)** *(Outcome: Online Purchase Intention, $N = 384$ survey + $N = 10$ interviews)*: This paper directly tests the same customer evaluation chain (`Information Quality & Credibility → Usefulness → Adoption → Purchase Intention`) across two competing touchpoints: **Social Media** vs. **Shopping Websites**. It proves that eWOM on shopping websites has a much stronger effect on purchase intention ($\beta = 0.358\text{–}0.430, p < 0.001$) than social media posts ($\beta \approx 0$), and supplies our exact 18-item questionnaire scale (Appendix Table A1) for touchpoint information quality, credibility, and usefulness.
9. **[George, Joseph, Abraham & Joseph (2023)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/George_et_al_2023_Brand_Trust_Engagement_Social_Commerce.pdf)** *(Outcome: Brand Trust [$R^2 = 0.383$], Brand Engagement [$R^2 = 0.311$], and Brand Usage Intention [$R^2 = 0.253$], $N = 625$ Indian Consumers)*: Conducted across 625 Indian users of WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, and YouTube, this Q1 study distinguishes between **Interactive Social Presence (chat/messaging)**, **Online Reviews & Ratings**, and **Social Media Likes/Shares**. It proves that superficial social media engagement (likes/shares) drives engagement ($\beta = 0.47$) more than trust ($\beta = 0.25$), whereas interactive chat ($\beta = 0.33$) and reviews ($\beta = 0.32$) are vital to build the **Brand Trust** required for conversion, and provides a 38-item questionnaire validated in India.
10. **[Roosdhani, Farida & Indriani (2023)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Roosdhani_et_al_2023_Digital_Marketing_Indonesian_SMEs.pdf)** *(Outcome: SME Customer Acquisition & Sales Performance, $N = 109$ SMEs)*: Directly titled *"From Likes to Sales,"* this study investigates how emerging-market SMEs translate social media activity into revenue. Using PLS-SEM, it demonstrates that social media marketing improves SME sales performance primarily **through Customer Acquisition ($\beta = 0.720, p < 0.001$)** rather than direct unmediated effects, while highlighting why customer-level touchpoint data is needed beyond owner self-reports.
11. **[Herhausen, Kleinlercher, Verhoef, Emrich & Rudolph (2019)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Herhausen_et_al_2019_Loyalty_Formation_Customer_Journey_Segments.pdf)** *(Outcome: Journey Satisfaction & Customer Loyalty / Repurchase vs. Switching Intention, $N = 5,092$ journeys (,443 + 2,649$))*: Published in the *Journal of Retailing*, this benchmark paper operationalizes **17 distinct pre-purchase and purchase touchpoints** (search engine, brand website, online reviews, price comparison, social media, email, mobile app, physical store, peer WOM) across 5 customer journey segments. It provides the gold-standard checklist of touchpoint exposure indicators while establishing our core gap: prior multi-touchpoint journey research has focused on **repeat-purchase loyalty at large retailers** rather than **first-time customer acquisition at SMEs**.
12. **[Hollebeek & Macky (2019)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Hollebeek_Macky_2019_Digital_Content_Marketing_Engagement_Trust_Value.pdf)** *(Outcome: Cognitive/Emotional/Behavioral Engagement → Brand Trust [Credibility & Benevolence] → Consumer Perceived Value)*: Published in the *Journal of Interactive Marketing*, this paper provides the exact theoretical architecture for the middle of our conceptual model. It explains how functional and hedonic digital touchpoint interactions trigger **Cognitive, Emotional, and Behavioral Engagement** (1st-tier), which subsequently build **Credibility and Benevolence Trust** (2nd-tier) and **Perceived Value** (3rd-tier), replacing older non-digital engagement papers.
13. **[Ismagilova, Slade, Rana & Dwivedi (2020)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Ismagilova_et_al_2020_eWOM_Intention_to_Buy_Meta_Analysis.pdf)** *(Outcome: Intention to Buy across 69 empirical studies, $N$ up to 29,593)*: This comprehensive meta-analysis synthesizes 69 empirical studies on earned peer touchpoints (eWOM/online reviews) and eliminates the need to cite half a dozen repetitive single-survey eWOM papers. It proves meta-analytically that **mere eWOM Volume is NOT statistically significant ($r = 0.245, p = 0.068$)**, whereas **Trust in the Message ($r = 0.506$)**, **Perceived Usefulness ($r = 0.496$)**, and **Argument Quality ($r = 0.449$)** are the true drivers of buying intention.
14. **[Baxendale, Macdonald & Wilson (2015)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Baxendale_et_al_2015_Impact_Different_Touchpoints_Brand_Consideration.pdf)** *(Outcome: Change in Brand Consideration across $N = 13,962$ journeys)*: This is the foundational *Journal of Retailing* touchpoint measurement study and cannot be replaced by newer papers because it pioneered the separation of **Touchpoint Exposure Frequency ($\ln(\text{Freq}+1)$)** from **Touchpoint Affective Positivity (`AvgPos`—perceived quality/valence of the encounter)**. It proves that incorporating encounter quality alongside touchpoint frequency nearly doubles explanatory power across brand-owned, peer WOM, and earned touchpoints.
15. **[Du, Zhong, Nair, Cui & Shou (2019)](file:///g:/marketing_paper/Du_et_al_2019_Causal_Multi_Touch_Attribution_1902.00215.pdf)** *(Outcome: Binary Conversion $Y \in \{0,1\}$ across 9 digital touchpoint channels)*: This methodological paper directly compares **Shapley Value (SHAP) multi-touch attribution**, **Logistic Regression**, and **Machine Learning (RNN)** models on customer touchpoint conversion paths. It provides the exact mathematical formulation for using **Shapley Values (SHAP)** to rank each digital touchpoint's marginal contribution to binary first-purchase conversion ($Y \in \{0,1\}$), while clearly distinguishing predictive attribution from causal incrementality.

## 5. MOST IMPORTANT FINAL QUESTION: THE MOST DEFENSIBLE RESEARCH GAP

Based **exclusively** on the 17 Core papers and the broader 60-paper repository evidence—without exaggeration or invented claims—our study addresses a **three-part, empirically verifiable research gap**:

### 1. The Outcome & Context Gap: SME Digital Marketing Research Stops at Firm-Level Adoption or Vanity Engagement Rather Than Customer-Level First Purchase
- **Evidence from Core Literature:** `Azhar & Juidette's (2025)` PRISMA systematic review of 263 studies (2015–2024) proves that only **12 studies globally** investigate digital marketing for **customer acquisition**, and only **1 qualitative study** (`Sedalo et al., 2022`) examined SMEs. Meanwhile, SME empirical studies (`Ahmad et al., 2019`; `Chatterjee & Kar, 2020`; `Roosdhani et al., 2023`) rely on **firm-owner self-reports** and reveal the **"SME Bandwagon Paradox"** (`Ahmad et al., 2019`, $\beta = 0.098$, n.s.): small businesses adopt social media and accumulate likes/shares ("vanity engagement"), yet cannot identify which specific customer-facing digital touchpoints actually convert a prospect into a first-time buyer.

### 2. The Touchpoint & Mechanism Gap: Prior Consumer Studies Either Isolate a Single Channel, Stop at Purchase Intention, or Omit Direct Conversational Touchpoints (WhatsApp/DM)
- **Evidence from Core Literature:**
  1. **Stopping at Intention vs. Actual Conversion:** While social media and eWOM studies routinely model **Purchase Intention** (`Erkan & Evans, 2018`; `Ismagilova et al., 2020`; `George et al., 2023`), `Dwivedi et al.'s (2023)` meta-analytic SEM across 68 studies proves a substantial **Intention–Behavior Gap** ($\beta = 0.28$ from intention to actual purchase) and shows that **Trust ($\beta = 0.33$)** directly drives actual conversion behavior.
  2. **Ignoring Conversational Touchpoints in Multi-Touchpoint Models:** Multi-touchpoint customer journey studies (`Baxendale et al., 2015`; `Herhausen et al., 2019`; `Hallikainen et al., 2019`; `Moliner & Tortosa-Edo, 2024`) examine large Western retailers and focus on *brand consideration* or *repeat loyalty*, omitting the **Direct Conversational Touchpoints (WhatsApp Business catalogs, 1-on-1 chat, Instagram DMs)** that `De (2025)` and `George et al. (2023)` prove are critical for building trust and closing first-time sales in Indian small businesses.

### 3. The Methodological / Attribution Gap: Clickstream Attribution Ignores Psychology, While Psychological SEM Ignores Multi-Touch Conversion Attribution
- **Evidence from Core Literature:** As `Buhalis & Volchek (2021, pp. 31–39)` demonstrate in their synthesis of 62 attribution studies, algorithmic multi-touch attribution models (`Du et al., 2019`) rely on enterprise clickstream logs that small businesses do not possess and treat touchpoints as mechanical click counts, ignoring *why* an interaction converts (through **Digital Engagement, Trust, and Perceived Value**, per `Hollebeek & Macky, 2019` and `Bleier et al., 2019`). Conversely, psychological SEM studies test latent constructs on purchase intention without attributing **actual binary first-purchase conversion ($Y \in \{0,1\}$)** across competing digital touchpoints (`Kim, Ferrin & Rao, 2008`).

### Summary Statement of Our Defensible Research Gap (Ready for Your Proposal / Guide)
> **“While small businesses increasingly rely on digital channels to attract customers, existing SME research predominantly examines firm-level technology adoption (`Ahmad et al., 2019`; `Chatterjee & Kar, 2020`) or stops at online purchase intention within a single channel (`Erkan & Evans, 2018`; `Ismagilova et al., 2020`), leaving a major gap identified by `Azhar & Juidette (2025)` regarding how digital marketing actually drives first-time customer acquisition. Furthermore, existing multi-touchpoint journey and attribution models focus on repeat loyalty or enterprise clickstream logs (`Herhausen et al., 2019`; `Buhalis & Volchek, 2021`), overlooking both the psychological evaluation mechanism (`Digital Engagement → Trust & Perceived Value`, `Hollebeek & Macky, 2019`; `Dwivedi et al., 2023`) and the direct conversational touchpoints (`WhatsApp Business / DMs`, `De, 2025`; `George et al., 2023`) upon which small businesses depend. Therefore, this study bridges marketing attribution and consumer psychology at the customer level by identifying which specific digital touchpoints—and which underlying engagement, trust, and value mechanisms—drive both purchase intention and actual first-time customer conversion ($Y \in \{0,1\}$) in small businesses.”**
