## 2. Literature Review — II

## 2. Literature Review — III
**Pillar 3 · Inside the Customer’s Mind: Engagement, Trust, Online Reviews, and Perceived Value.** Touchpoints only lead to a purchase when they move a customer from surface engagement (likes/views) to real trust and perceived value.

*Table 3 — papers 10–13 of 17*

| Paper name | Author(s) & ID | Year | Method | Key contribution | Gap identified |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Digital Content Marketing’s Role in Fostering Engagement, Trust, and Value** — The step-by-step psychology chain | Hollebeek & Macky [10]<br>J. Interactive Marketing, 45, 27–41<br>DOI: 10.1016/j.intmar.2018.07.003 | 2019 | Conceptual Framework combining Customer Engagement Theory and the Elaboration Likelihood Model | Builds the exact 3-step chain used in our study: Digital Content/Touchpoint Interaction → Customer Engagement (Step 1) → Brand Trust (Step 2) → Perceived Value (Step 3) | Purely theoretical paper — has no survey data and does not test which digital touchpoints trigger this chain |
| **Social Media or Shopping Websites?** — Why website reviews beat social media posts for buying | Erkan & Evans [11]<br>J. | **Brand Trust and Engagement in Social Commerce** — How Indian shoppers react to likes, reviews, and chat | George, Joseph, Abraham & Joseph [13]<br>Int. J. ## 2. Literature Review — IV
**Pillar 4 · From "Intention to Buy" to "Actual First Purchase" & Multi-Touch Analytics.** Many people intend to buy but never pay (β = 0.28), and big-data click models ignore customer trust—so we must combine Psychology (PLS-SEM) with Binary Conversion Analytics (Logistic Regression & SHAP).

*Table 4 — papers 14–17 of 17*

| Paper name | Author(s) & ID | Year | Method | Key contribution | Gap identified |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A Trust-Based Consumer Decision-Making Model in E-Commerce** — Testing both Intention AND Actual Buying (Y = 1 vs. 0) | Kim, Ferrin & Rao [14]<br>Decision Support Systems, 44(2), 544–564<br>DOI: 10.1016/j.dss.2007.07.001 | 2008 | Two-Stage Study: Stage 1 PLS-SEM (468 shoppers) + Stage 2 Binary Logistic Regression comparing 261 Actual Buyers (Y=1) vs. 207 Non-Buyers (Y=0) | Proves Information Quality and Reputation build Trust and Perceived Value/Benefit (β = 0.355), which drive Purchase Intention (β = 0.314) and predict who actually buys (Y=1) vs. who drops off (Y=0) | Written in 2008 for a single website — does not include modern social media or WhatsApp/DM touchpoints |
| **A Meta-Analytic SEM for Understanding Social Commerce Adoption** — Proof of the Intention–Behavior Gap | Dwivedi et al.
